Scheduling Algorithms

Shortest Job First (SJF)

SJF selects the waiting process with the smallest execution time to execute next. It can be non-preemptive or preemptive (Shortest Remaining Time First).

Round Robin (RR)

RR assigns a fixed time quantum per process in a cyclic order, ensuring fairness and starvation-free execution.
